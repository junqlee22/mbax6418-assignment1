Why do they diverge?

The LLM reads the intent of the review — "perfect gift!" registers as joy. The NRC lexicon scores individual words: "gift", "give", "perfect" are tagged as anticipation in the NRC database, skewing the word-list results heavily. The two methods agree on only 27/150 reviews (18%).
